FAQ

Good questions.

Does the gear work?

Yes. Bought in October 2026 and used for about 10 episodes, but kept like new: the protective film is still on the case, everything works, and it’s all fully charged.

Can I buy the gear without the website?

The lot is sold as one piece for $500. If you don’t need a site, the website can be gifted to someone you choose: a business, a family member, a community group.

What kind of website do I get?

A custom site designed by BakeAWeb from your own content and photos, built to the same standard we charge $1,200 for. We’ll walk you through it at kickoff.

Is the podcast still running?

No. After about 10 published episodes, The Owl’s Lens is on hold while we build A Web. That makes this the show’s one original kit.

What’s it good for?

Plug-and-play recording for podcasts, YouTube, TikTok, Zoom or interviews. Two wireless lav mics clip on, the phone mounts on the tripod, the LED light sits on top.

Will it be worth more someday?

We hope so, and we’re working hard to make A Web a story worth collecting. But nobody can promise a collectible’s future value, so please buy it because you want it.
